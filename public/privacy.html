IGB Mobile – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

IGB Mobile ("the app") is provided by IGB Accounting ("IGB", "we", "us") for our clients to submit receipts and view their bookkeeping records. This policy explains what information the app collects and how it is used.

Information we collect

  • Account information – your name, email address and login details. If you sign in with Google or Facebook, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from that service.
  • Business records – receipts, invoices and documents you scan or upload, and the sales, purchase, expense, receivable and payable records we keep for your business.
  • Camera and photos – used only when you choose to scan or upload a document.
  • Face ID / Touch ID – handled entirely by your device. We never receive or store your biometric data.
  • Microphone – used only if you use voice chat features.

How we use your information

  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To process your documents and provide bookkeeping, accounting and tax compliance services.
  • To show your dashboards, reports and filing deadlines.
  • To contact you about your account and our services.

We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising or tracking.

Sharing

Your information is shared only with IGB staff assigned to your account, with service providers that host and store our data on our behalf, and with government agencies (such as the BIR) when required by law or for filings you have engaged us to make.

Storage and security

Your data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored on secured servers. Access is limited to authorized personnel.

Retention and deletion

We keep your records for as long as you are our client and as long as required by Philippine tax and accounting laws. You may ask us to delete your account and personal information by contacting us below; records we are legally required to keep will be retained until that period ends.

Your rights

Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173) you may access, correct or request deletion of your personal information, and object to its processing. Contact us to exercise these rights.

Children

The app is intended for businesses and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version will always be on this page.
